Digital Automation & Robotics, Open to Everyone

Starlight Robotics is a volunteer-led initiative building web resources and knowledge products around history and facts, collecting knowledge and composing it into structured and usable reference points.

We currently maintain 12 live sub-projects:

  • Starlight Tools
  • Starlight Longevity
  • Daily Life History
  • Surname Origin
  • Historia Medica
  • Sport Rules
  • Geography Atlas
  • Hobby Portal
  • Grow Blueprint
  • World of Creatures
  • Automobile History
  • Flag Record

Follow our progress on Instagram, X and join the community at r/starlightrobotics.
